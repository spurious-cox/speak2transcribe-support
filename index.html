Speak2Transcribe turns speech into text on your Mac — from the microphone or from whatever your Mac is playing — and can type it straight into the app you're using.

Looking for the open-source Python version? See MyTranscribe.

Getting started

Choose Mic or Mac, then click Capture. Words appear as they're heard. Click Stop to finish. The first Capture downloads Apple's on-device speech model once.

Type at cursor

Check Floating to switch to the small panel, then check Type at cursor. Click into a text field in another app and Capture: each phrase is typed there. Say “new line” or “new paragraph” for line breaks.

Permissions

In System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security, turn on Speak2Transcribe under:

  • Microphone — for Mic
  • Screen & System Audio Recording — for Mac
  • Device Control and Data Access (Accessibility on earlier macOS) — for Type at cursor

After turning one on, quit and reopen Speak2Transcribe.

Help in the app

Choose Help ▸ Speak2Transcribe Help (⌘?), or hover over any control for a tip.
