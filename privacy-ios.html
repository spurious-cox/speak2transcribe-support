Speak2Transcribe for iPhone · DRAFT, effective date to be set at release

Summary

Speak2Transcribe does not collect, store, share or sell any information about you. Your voice and your text stay on your iPhone. The only network use is an optional one-time model download, described below.

Audio and text

Audio from your microphone is transcribed on your iPhone by Apple's on-device speech recognition. The audio is not recorded or saved. The text is shown in the app and saved automatically as a text file in the folder you choose in Files (or in the app's own Transcripts folder on your iPhone). Those files are yours; the app does not upload or share them.

Keyboard and Full Access

The Speak2Transcribe keyboard types the text you dictate into the app in front of you. It asks for Full Access only so it can receive that text from the Speak2Transcribe app on the same iPhone. The two share it locally through an App Group. The keyboard does not read what you type, does not log keystrokes, and makes no network connections.

Speaker labels (optional)

If you turn on Label speakers, the app uses an open-source speaker-separation model that runs entirely on your iPhone to mark who is speaking, for example "Speaker 1". The first time you turn this on, the app downloads the model once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). That download is an ordinary web request, so Hugging Face may see your IP address and the time of the request, as with any download. No audio, text or other data about you is sent. After the download, speaker labels work without a connection. If you leave the setting off, the app downloads nothing.

Speech model download

The first time you use dictation, iOS may download Apple's speech model. That download is handled by iOS, not by this app.

Microphone and background use

The microphone is used only while you have a session running, so dictation can continue while you switch to another app. iOS shows its microphone indicator whenever the microphone is in use.

No accounts, tracking or analytics

There is no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Settings

Your choices, such as which folder to save in and the formatting options, are kept in the app's settings on your iPhone.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.
