Speak2Transcribe for Mac · Effective November 4, 2026

Summary

Speak2Transcribe does not collect, store, share or sell any information about you. Everything happens on your Mac.

Audio and text

Audio from your microphone, or from what your Mac is playing, is transcribed on your Mac by Apple's on-device speech recognition. The audio is not recorded or saved. The text stays in the app until you clear it, and is saved to a file only when you choose Save. Neither is sent anywhere.

Type at cursor

When you turn on Type at cursor, the app types your own transcribed words into the app in front of you. It does not read, record or collect anything from other apps, and it does not use the clipboard.

Screen & System Audio Recording

This permission is used only to hear what your Mac is playing. No pictures of your screen are captured or kept.

No accounts, tracking or analytics

There is no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app makes no network connections of its own. The one-time download of Apple's speech model is handled by macOS.

Settings

Your choices, such as Mic or Mac and window positions, are kept in the app's preferences on your Mac.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
